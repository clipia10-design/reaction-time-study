Your privacy
  • No login, name, email or contact details are needed. You are recorded only as an anonymous code (“Person 1”, “Person 2”, …). You must be 18 or older to take part.
  • Only your answers to the four questions above, your 3 real-test times, whether you were interrupted, the input type your browser reports (mouse or touch) and the date/time of the test are saved. Practice rounds are not saved, and the study does not record your IP address.
  • Results are stored in a private spreadsheet that only the researcher can open. The service is hosted by Google, which handles technical connection data under its own privacy policy.
  • You only ever see averages, never anyone else's individual results. You can withdraw (delete) your result right after the test.

About this study

This is a student research project for the Statistics 2 course at University College Groningen. We study whether simple reaction time is related to age, and whether it differs with regular physical activity and the device used, while taking last night’s sleep into account.

Our expectations are that reaction time increases with age, that more active people react faster, and that touchscreens give slightly slower times than a mouse or trackpad. Results from a single test vary a lot from moment to moment, so your score is not a measure of your health or abilities.

Please don’t tell others who still have to take the test what we expect — it could influence their answers.
